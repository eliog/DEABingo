LibDataBroker-1.1 by tekkub
https://github.com/tekkub/libdatabroker-1-1
https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/libdatabroker-1-1

LibDataBroker is a shared library written to be embedded in World of Warcraft
addons, and is embedded unmodified here as its documentation describes. The
repository carries no licence file; its CurseForge project page shows the
site's default "All Rights Reserved". The library's author has distributed
it for exactly this use since 2008 and it ships inside a large share of all
published addons. Copyright remains with the author.
